Free page review vs. a paid CRO audit

The free page review is a focused, personal review of one page — the likely momentum-killers and the three highest-impact fixes. A CRO audit is a comprehensive paid engagement: deeper analysis against your traffic and tracking, a prioritized fix list, and quick-win implementation. Start with the free review; we'll tell you if a full audit is warranted.

hello@pagedriven.com
How is this different from the free page review?

The free page review is a short, personal look at one page — where it's most likely losing momentum and the three highest-impact fixes. A CRO audit is a comprehensive paid engagement: full page and funnel review against traffic data, prioritized findings, and quick-win implementation where scope allows.

Do you need analytics access?

It helps. Where analytics or session data is available we use it; where it isn't, the audit still works — it's just grounded in message, structure, and heuristics rather than behavior data.

Will you tell us to rebuild?

Only if the findings justify it. Many audits end with a short list of fixes to the existing page, and we'll say so plainly when that's the answer.

Do you run A/B tests as part of an audit?

No. Experimentation depends on sufficient traffic and reliable tracking to produce readable results. Where that's in place, we can recommend what to test — as part of ongoing optimization, not a one-off audit.

Who actually does the audit?

Riz personally leads the review and the findings report — every audit, no exceptions.
