Using visitor behavior to strengthen six service pages.

Levitate Media already had a mature service-page system with strong work samples, proof and process content. The challenge

The pages were strong. The opportunity was to make the strongest content work harder.

Visitor behavior showed a clear preference for visual proof, particularly video examples, case studies and the production process. The opportunity was not to rebuild the page system, but to give these high-value elements greater prominence, reduce avoidable friction and improve how visitors moved toward a quote request.

Key findings

Video examples led attention

Work samples consistently attracted the strongest engagement, showing that buyers wanted to evaluate production quality early.

Video case studies strengthened proof

On the 2D and 3D pages, video case studies aligned naturally with how visitors preferred to assess the work.

The process remained valuable

The production-process timeline continued to earn attention even when it appeared further down the page.

Useful content was sometimes buried

FAQs and other decision-support content received meaningful engagement when visitors reached it, suggesting an opportunity to improve discoverability.

CTA treatment could be tightened

CTA wording and width varied across some pages, creating an opportunity for more consistent presentation.

Trust deserved stronger presentation

Existing client volume, ratings and recognizable logos could be surfaced in a more concise and visually prominent trust strip.

Recommended page sequence

Turning behavioral patterns into a clearer page journey.

Recommendations

Evidence-led refinements for emphasis, scannability and trust.

  • Feature the strongest video examples first instead of displaying too many simultaneously.
  • Add industry filters to make relevant work easier to find.
  • Make video examples manageable through the CMS.
  • Redesign dense text sections into more scannable cards supported by relevant visuals.
  • Give the pink/red brand palette a more intentional role in calls to action and emphasis.
  • Use a curated set of recognizable logos rather than an oversized logo collection.
  • Consider section-aware navigation to improve access to FAQs, case studies and other valuable lower-page content.
  • Preserve high-performing elements such as the process timeline and featured testimonial.
Outcome

From behavioral evidence to a prioritized optimization roadmap.

Levitate received a cross-page CRO plan grounded in the sections visitors were already engaging with. The recommendations preserved the existing strengths of the service pages while improving emphasis, scannability, trust presentation, CTA treatment and access to valuable lower-page content.
