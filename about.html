The founder story

From product leadership to PageDriven.

Before founding PageDriven, Riz spent years in product leadership, working on how people move through digital products, where they hesitate, and what gets them to act. The pattern was always the same: teams invested heavily in getting visitors to the door, then under-invested in what those visitors found when they arrived.

PageDriven exists to close that gap. Instead of treating a website as a design artifact, we treat it as the working part of a traffic investment: planned around the visitor's intent, written around the offer, and built to be measured.

That product background shapes every engagement. Start with the user and the goal, then let structure, copy, and design follow.

    The conversion philosophy

    Principles that shape every build.

    01

    Traffic first,
    page second

    02

    before styling

    per page
